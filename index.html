Privacy Policy – FD Office

Last updated: October 9, 2026

FD Office ("the App") is developed and provided by FD OMZ. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use FD Office.

1. Files and Documents

FD Office allows users to open, view, create, edit, and manage supported office documents and files (such as DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, CSV, TXT and PDF). Files selected or created by the user are processed locally on the device to provide the requested functionality. FD Office does not upload the contents of your documents to FD OMZ or to any server, and does not sell users' documents or personal files.

When you open a file from another app ("Open with" or "Share"), the App stores a temporary working copy in its private cache so it can be displayed or edited. Your original file is not modified; edits to complex documents are saved as a new copy or to a location you choose. Recent-file references, language, theme and save-location preferences are stored only on your device and can be cleared from the App's Settings or by removing the App's data.

When you use Android's Share or Print action, the document is provided only to the app or printer you choose. Links inside documents are not opened automatically; you may copy a link and open it yourself.

2. File Access

The App accesses only files or documents that you select with Android's system file picker or that you send to the App from another app. Access is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user.

3. Personal Information

FD Office does not require users to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. FD Office has no analytics or tracking system of its own. The developer does not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Advertising

FD Office is free and displays advertisements (a banner and occasional full-screen ads) provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process information from your device, such as the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and operating-system information, information about ad interactions, and crash and diagnostic data, in accordance with Google's policies. This information does not include the contents of your documents.

Consent and your choices. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. If consent is not given, ads are not requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in the App under Settings > Ad privacy settings (shown where the form is required). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Play Services

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies. Google AdMob is the only third-party service that receives data from the App.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

FD OMZ does not hold your documents or personal data on its own servers, so there is nothing for FD OMZ to retain or delete on your behalf. Data stored on your device by the App (temporary copies, recent-file list and preferences) is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Files you save remain in the location you chose until you delete them. Data held by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies; see the link in Section 4 and your Google account's ad settings.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by the App; documents are processed on your device and are not transmitted by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

FD Office is a general-purpose productivity application and is not specifically designed for children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the App.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App, applicable requirements, or services used by the App change. Updates will be published on this page together with the revised update date.
