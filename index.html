Privacy Policy – FD Document Viewer Last updated: November 24, 2026 FD Document Viewer ("the App"), Android package name com.fdomz.fdofficeviewer, is developed and provided by FD OMZ. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. 1. Files and Documents FD Document Viewer allows users to open, view, create, edit, and manage supported document formats, including DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, CSV, TXT, and PDF. Some formats and advanced editing features have limited support. Files you select or create are processed locally on your device to provide document functionality. The App does not automatically upload the contents of your documents to FD OMZ servers or send document contents to advertising services. When you open a file from another application using "Open with" or "Share", the App may store a temporary working copy in its private cache so that the file can be viewed or edited. Viewing does not intentionally modify the original file; when editing is supported, a separate copy may be saved or you may choose a destination. References to recently opened files and preferences such as language, theme, and save location are stored locally. When you use Android's Share or Print action, the document is provided to the application, recipient, or printer you choose. Those third parties handle the shared document according to their own practices. The App does not automatically open links found inside documents. 2. File Access The App accesses documents selected using Android's system file picker or sent from other applications through supported Android sharing and opening mechanisms. This access is used for document functionality requested by the user. Files are not routinely scanned or uploaded to an FD OMZ document-processing server. 3. Personal Information FD Document Viewer does not require an account and does not request your name, email address, or telephone number for normal document use. FD OMZ does not run a separate account-based analytics service within the App and does not sell users' personal information. Third-party advertising services may process device and interaction data as described below. 4. Advertising The App is free and includes Google AdMob advertising functionality. Depending on the version and configuration, the App may display banner advertisements and occasional full-screen advertisements; development builds may use test advertisements. When AdMob is enabled, Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers and other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device and operating-system information, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and security. The App does not intentionally provide your document contents to AdMob. Consent and your choices. Where applicable privacy rules require it, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present choices and manage advertising consent. Ad requests are subject to the current consent status and applicable Google requirements. Depending on your choices and the applicable rules, advertisements may be limited, non-personalized, or unavailable. Where a privacy options entry is required and available, you can review or change your choice under Settings > Ad privacy settings. Android settings may also offer advertising ID controls. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. 5. See Google's Privacy Policy. 7. Data Retention and Deletion FD OMZ does not maintain a server-side repository of your documents. Temporary working copies, recent-file references, and preferences may remain in the App's local storage until cleared by the App or by Android. Clearing App storage or uninstalling generally removes data from the App's private storage. Files saved to locations you choose may remain after uninstalling and must be deleted separately. Android backup or device-transfer functionality may handle some locally stored settings, subject to the device's configuration. Data held by Google for advertising is governed by Google's retention and deletion practices and applicable privacy controls. 8. Data Security The App uses local processing for supported document functionality and applies protections to externally supplied files. Reasonable measures are taken to protect the information the App handles. Third-party services may transfer information for advertising and related purposes. No method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure. 9. FD OMZ does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through its own servers. When ads are enabled, applicable age-related advertising requirements apply. 10.
