Privacy Policy – FD Office

Last updated: November 14, 2026

FD Office and FD Office - Document Viewer (collectively referred to as "the Apps") are developed and provided by FD OMZ. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use either application.

This Privacy Policy applies to the following Android applications:

  • FD Office
    Package name: com.fdomz.fdoffice
  • FD Office - Document Viewer
    Package name: com.fdomz.fdofficeviewer

1. Files and Documents

The Apps allow users to open, view, create, edit, and manage supported office documents and files, including DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, CSV, TXT, PDF, and other supported formats. Files selected or created by users are processed locally on their devices to provide the requested functionality.

FD OMZ does not upload the contents of your documents to its own servers, and does not sell users' documents or personal files.

When you open a file from another application using "Open with" or "Share", the Apps may store a temporary working copy in their private cache so the file can be displayed or edited.

Your original file is not intentionally modified during viewing. When editing complex documents, changes may be saved as a new copy or to a location you choose, depending on the supported functionality.

Recent-file references, language preferences, theme settings, and save-location preferences are stored locally on your device. These preferences can be cleared using available application settings or by clearing the application's data.

When you use Android's Share or Print functionality, the document is provided to the application or printer you select. The Apps do not automatically upload your documents to FD OMZ.

Links contained inside documents are not automatically opened by the Apps. You may choose to copy or open links yourself.

2. File Access

The Apps access files and documents selected by users through Android's system file picker or shared with the Apps by another application.

File access is used to provide document viewing, editing, saving, and management functions requested by users.

The Apps do not intentionally scan or upload unrelated personal documents to FD OMZ servers.

3. Personal Information

The Apps do not require users to create an account and do not directly request their name, email address, or telephone number for normal document functionality.

FD OMZ does not operate an independent analytics or behavioral tracking service within the Apps.

The developer does not sell personal information to third parties.

Third-party advertising services may process certain device and usage information, as explained in the Advertising section below.

4. Advertising

The Apps are provided free of charge and may display advertisements through Google AdMob, including banner advertisements and occasional full-screen advertisements.

Depending on the application version, configuration, and availability, advertising may be enabled, disabled, or use test advertising during development.

When Google AdMob is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Android advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Device and operating system information
  • Advertisement interactions
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information

This information may be used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and related advertising services, subject to Google's applicable policies.

The Apps do not intentionally provide the contents of users' documents to Google AdMob for advertising.

Consent and your choices. Where consent is required by applicable law, including in relevant European jurisdictions, the Apps use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent.

Where the applicable consent requirements are not satisfied, the Apps do not request advertisements that require such consent.

Where required, users can review or change their advertising privacy choices through the application's Settings > Ad privacy settings option.

Users may also manage advertising preferences and available advertising ID controls through their Android device settings.

More information about Google's data practices: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

7. Data Retention and Deletion

FD OMZ does not store users' documents or account information on its own servers. Therefore, FD OMZ does not maintain server-side copies of those documents for deletion.

Temporary files, recent-file references, and application preferences stored within an application's private storage can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling it.

Files saved to user-selected locations may remain on the device after uninstalling the application and must be deleted separately by the user.

Information processed by Google for advertising and related services is subject to Google's data retention and deletion policies.

8. Data Security

The Apps use local device processing for supported document functionality. The developer takes reasonable measures to avoid unauthorized access to or unnecessary transmission of document contents.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should protect their devices, use trusted applications, and avoid sharing sensitive files with unknown third-party services.

9. Children's Privacy

The Apps are general-purpose productivity applications and are not specifically designed for children.

FD OMZ does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through its own servers.

Where advertising is enabled, applicable age-related advertising and privacy requirements must be followed.
